Privacy policy

Applies to the Fishbowl Relay app and the live gallery at yycgame.l33.ca · Updated November 2026

What Fishbowl Relay is

Fishbowl Relay is a private Android app used by Fishbowl Photography to move photos from a camera to a Google Drive folder, adding a watermark on the way. It is installed only on the photographer's own phone and signs in only to the photographer's own Google account.

What it accesses in Google Drive

The app requests the drive.file permission. That lets it create one folder in the signed-in account's Drive and upload photos into it. It cannot see, read, change or delete any other file in that Drive.

What is shared

The folder the app creates is shared as "anyone with the link can view" so that the live gallery page can display the photos. The gallery is a static web page that reads that folder through Google's public Drive API. No one other than Google receives the photos.

What is collected

  • No analytics, advertising identifiers, or tracking of any kind, in the app or on the gallery.
  • No personal data is sent to Fishbowl Photography or any third party. Google's own processing of Drive files is covered by Google's privacy policy.
  • The gallery remembers a viewer's light or dark theme choice in their own browser only.

Removing access

Access granted to the app can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions. Deleting the Drive folder removes all uploaded photos.
